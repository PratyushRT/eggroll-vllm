## Check 3 — Calibration conditioned on retention status

Key signal is the `regressed` row (problems step-0 got right, step t gets wrong). `conf_wrong_delta` = conf on wrong samples at step t minus conf on wrong samples at step 0, within that group.

- **Negative delta** → calibrated forgetting (model became humble about new misses)
- **Positive delta** → local reward-hacking (still confident despite newly wrong)
